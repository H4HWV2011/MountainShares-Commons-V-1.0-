# Document Classification Model

Documents are classified into five categories:

1. Foundation
2. Governance
3. Legal
4. Economics
5. Programs
6. Commerce
7. Technology
8. Operations
9. Research / Appendices

---

# Authority Levels

## Level 1 — Constitutional

Defines foundational principles and identity.

Examples:
- Mission
- Values
- Governance philosophy


## Level 2 — Policy

Defines mandatory rules.

Examples:
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Use
- Program Rules


## Level 3 — Specification

Defines system behavior.

Examples:
- Economic Safety Specification
- Technical Architecture


## Level 4 — Procedure

Defines operational execution.

Examples:
- Onboarding Checklist
- Moderation Procedures


## Level 5 — Reference

Provides explanation or history.

Examples:
- Business Plans
- Research Papers
- Thesis Chapters
